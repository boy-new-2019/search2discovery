# Known limitations and scientific integrity

- **Not a science oracle.** Keyword/abstract classification, even after native review, may miss mechanism-related papers; novelty searches cannot exhaust the literature. Scientific users must verify crucial claims.
- **Not an experiment runner.** R3 reviews published empirical evidence and unexecuted thought scenarios. Experiment design is planned-only; no training, benchmark evaluation, simulation or independently generated numeric outcomes.
- **Source limitations.** OpenAlex metadata is not a peer-reviewed proof. Not all OA PDFs are downloadable or machine-readable. OCR/images/formulas/tables often require separate visual human checking; string-matched quotes do not verify their interpretation.
- **Review independence.** Offline fixture tests do not prove independent live model threads or that model names are accessible; independent reviewer consensus does not prove originality.
- **Official rankings.** Annual source check does not grant access to licensed individual JCR quartiles; CCF levels require matching official directory entries; CAS historical categories must not be represented as annually updated ranks.
- **Resource costs.** Local agent-task limits and input-character budgets are approximate safeguards, not actual billing metrics. Five mandatory reading rounds can extend across multiple usage windows. English prompts may be more concise but do not guarantee lower token cost.
- **Not fully autonomous.** The program does not guarantee one-session completion; it pauses at permission, native-agent, quota, provenance, or coverage gates. It does not autonomously run a 24/7 background daemon.
- **Prompt injection.** PDFs and webpages are untrusted data; the operating contract instructs agents to ignore embedded commands, but tool permissions and sandbox configuration must also be restricted by users.
- **Distribution rights.** Repository maintainers must verify the rights to release historical code, citations and logos; third-party papers and training data are not included.
