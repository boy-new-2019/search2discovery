# Committed round reports and high-value cycle gating

After S1, S2, each R1–R5 fulltext round and each MAP–FINAL cognition phase, deterministically refresh the independently committed Markdown/JSON report and `INDEX.md`. Record novel source anchors, open questions, traceable original file and task hashes, reviewer identities, pending visual checks and evidence overlap diagnostics. Generating reports must use no extra language-model call. An exact duplicate task must not create another report or charge an additional local dispatch reservation. If raw evidence/receipts change, fail closed rather than silently overwriting history.

After a complete cognition cycle, compare authenticated evidence and question state with the prior baseline. New bibliography records alone are insufficient to trigger another nine-agent cycle; a substantive question or new fulltext source requires a reasoned relevance judgment. Preserve all five original-text reading rounds and vetoes for core papers. Lexical novelty is a diagnostic only, never proof of scientific information gain.

Reports are **internal and English**. Only the final consolidated user-facing research presentation is Chinese.
