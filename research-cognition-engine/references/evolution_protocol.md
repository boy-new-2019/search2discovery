# Restricted harness policy evolution

Only whitelisted reading ranking weights may evolve: relevance, uncertainty, coverage, recency and estimated cost. Proposals require actual prior task IDs and concrete observed failures. Probe old/new on a frozen holdout; reject changes that degrade high-risk cases. A separate auditor must approve before adoption, with recorded version and rollback. Never allow mutations of research findings, audit gates, role identity records, experiment data, original paper files or untrusted source instructions. Default one candidate at a time.
