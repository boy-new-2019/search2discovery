# Topic-to-plan operating workflow

Start with the research topic, a frozen `goal.md` and an actual independent topic-cartographer's reviewed JSON. Produce validated English search concepts and related branches; classify authentic OpenAlex results by the topic, not legacy driving-specific keywords. Record venue candidates and their official authority evidence separately. Download only public OA PDFs and keep missing fulltext as a gap. Run original S1/S2 and R1–R5 for qualified core papers; R3 means published experimental evidence and qualitative unexecuted scenario reasoning only. Sync verified observations into research memory, generate questions, search closest prior art, perform independent novelty reviews and create design-only falsifiable experimental blueprints. Stop when evidence is insufficient; never force a final recommendation. Chinese is used only for the final researcher-facing presentation.
