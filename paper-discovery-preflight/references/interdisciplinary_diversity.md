# Interdisciplinary discovery and diversity

The target inspiration pool contains at least 50 distinct papers across at least 24 fine disciplines and 12 of the 15 macro discipline families, with at least 24 papers from relatively distant fields. Limit any fine field to three and any macro family to seven unless a specific reviewed exception is recorded. Rank real, high-quality recent sources before reproducibly randomizing within comparable categories. Never fabricate niche works, awards or research results to satisfy quotas.
